# Selected visual direction

## Decision

Use **Cabinet of Curiosities** as the primary visual direction, grounded by the hierarchy, whitespace, and restraint of **Living Editorial**.

Elias ranked the concepts on 2026-09-14:

1. Cabinet of Curiosities
2. Living Editorial
3. Signals and Systems

Cabinet felt like “an alive version of Living Editorial with a really nice personal layer and touch on top.” Living Editorial felt strong but less personal. Signals and Systems felt too harsh to use as the site-wide environment.

## Design rules

- Begin with clear editorial hierarchy and readable composition.
- Add life through tactile objects, real artifacts, selective colour, slight irregularity, and direct motion.
- Keep personality intentional; avoid turning every section into a novelty object.
- Let books, posters, photographs, project captures, records, and small data objects retain their own material character.
- Use technical grids, traces, and state indicators only inside relevant GitHub, training, fantasy, and systems content.
- Soften technical data treatments with the site's typography, spacing, colour, and human commentary.
- Preserve straightforward navigation, visible actions, mobile clarity, keyboard access, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Evaluation question

For each new section, ask whether the editorial structure is already clear before adding a tactile or playful layer. If the layer does not reveal personality, meaning, or interaction, leave it out.
