- `public/lanyard/webbing.webp` and `public/lanyard/foil.webp` — seamless 256 × 256 material textures for the 3D staff pass ([#486](https://github.com/3ixas/eliasb.dev/issues/486)), derived from samples generated with Codex ImageGen on 17 November 2026 ([#484](https://github.com/3ixas/eliasb.dev/issues/484), `docs/review/2026-11-17-generated-imagery/samples/{webbing,foil}.webp`), phase-locked to exact weave/wave repeat cells, illumination-flattened and cosine-blended across both wrap axes:
  - `webbing.webp` — prompt: *"Create one square, at least 1024 × 1024, seamless repeatable PBR base-colour tile of neutral mid-grey woven polyester ID lanyard webbing. Use strictly monochrome grey with equal red, green and blue. Fill the frame edge to edge with a fine, regular, low-contrast polyester twill, with vertical warp threads, visible fine yarn fibres and a faint satin sheen. Orthographic overhead macro view under uniform diffuse, shadowless light. The square is one complete repeat cell with an integer number of weave repeats along each axis. Threads that exit the right or bottom edge continue at the same position and phase on the left or top edge, with no change to pitch, brightness or orientation at the wrap. No ribbon outline, border, folds, vignette, logos, text, people, brand marks, or watermarks."*
  - `foil.webp` — prompt: *"Create one square, at least 1024 × 1024, seamless repeatable material texture of holographic security foil for a fictional staff ID card in three.js. Edge-to-edge straight overhead macro photograph under uniform soft lighting. Cover a silver base in a very fine, regular guilloche pattern of interlaced shallow waves and an even, subdued diffraction rainbow sheen. Use many small, identical repeats with an integer number of periods both horizontally and vertically. Compose as one periodic pattern tile: each fine line flows through the frame boundary and resumes at the corresponding position and angle on the opposite edge, and colour and illumination continue cleanly across each wrap. No large gradients or isolated highlights. No patch outline, symbols, logos, text, people, brand marks, or watermarks."*
