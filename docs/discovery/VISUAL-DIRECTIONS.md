# Visual direction territories

These three territories are approved for exploration. They must use the same confirmed content hierarchy so the comparison tests visual and interaction choices rather than different product scopes.

## 1. Living Editorial

### Intended feeling

Assured, thoughtful, warm, and carefully authored.

### Visual ingredients

- Strong editorial typography and generous composition.
- Primarily ink, white, and soft neutral surfaces with selective vivid colour.
- Fine rules, marginal notes, captions, and small typographic surprises.
- A real photograph may appear as a confident editorial portrait or a quieter About image.

### Motion

- The signature typed entrance resolves into the page.
- Sections arrive with restrained rhythm and depth.
- Motion supports hierarchy and continuity rather than decoration.

### Main risk

Becoming a tasteful but familiar designer portfolio. Personal artifacts and integrations must supply specificity.

## 2. Cabinet of Curiosities

### Intended feeling

Inviting, tactile, surprising, and unmistakably personal.

### Visual ingredients

- Books, movie posters, records, project artifacts, photographs, and data objects arranged as a considered collection.
- Each object retains its own colour against a quiet neutral environment.
- Layering, depth, page turns, sleeves, drawers, or other physical metaphors used selectively.

### Motion

- Objects respond with direct, understandable interactions.
- The experience rewards exploration without hiding basic navigation or content.

### Main risk

Novelty overwhelming clarity or creating excessive asset and animation work.

## 3. Signals and Systems

### Intended feeling

Precise, alive, intelligent, and quietly technical.

### Visual ingredients

- Timelines, traces, status indicators, diagrams, and data visualizations.
- A restrained grid and monochrome base with functional colour signals.
- Technical language translated into accessible visual stories.

### Motion

- Live signals update or resolve with state-aware transitions.
- System diagrams and timelines reveal relationships progressively.

### Main risk

Feeling like a dashboard or monitoring product instead of a personal site.

## Evaluation rule

Each direction must be assessed for clarity, distinctiveness, personal specificity, emotional warmth, mobile behaviour, accessibility, performance, and maintainability. Do not merge the concepts until each has been evaluated on its own terms.

## Comparison artifact

Design the same representative slice in every direction:

1. Signature entrance and introduction.
2. One selected project.
3. The current-life glimpse.
4. One Library interaction.

Produce desktop and mobile compositions first, then a focused coded prototype for motion and interaction. Use real project screenshots, one or two candidate photographs, and representative books, films, and music so the evaluation reflects Elias's actual material.

Both light and dark modes should be intentional. Follow the visitor's system preference initially and retain a manual choice. Every concept must preserve core content without advanced motion and provide reduced-motion behavior.
